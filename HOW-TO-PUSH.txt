STOREFRONT AUDIT (b158)
   Your catalogue: open the green "Your catalogue" bar at the top of Storefront audits and paste your
   inventory (Seller Central > Inventory > Manage All Inventory > export, or the All Listings report -
   paste the whole thing). Everything on it is marked "You sell this" on every rival's shelf.
   Archive: hover a rival card > the box icon (top right). Undo in the message; "Show them" at the
   bottom brings any back.
   Joint rule: on your storefront = Joint. After it leaves, a rival that adds it within 45 days = Joint
   automatically; a rival that adds it after your 45 days = new, to check ("You had it until ... · they added it ...").
   Change the 45 in the green "Your storefront" bar (14 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 days / always).
   Not lead / Missed it / Unsure come back to check after 90 days ("You said Not lead 95 days ago · check again").
   Discord / WS / Diff method / Joint last.
   New answer: 7 = Diff method (wholesale / PL - not your model), then Q / W / E for Wholesale / PL / Other.
   Bulk on a rival: set a filter (e.g. Sells: Under 20 a month) > "Tick all N shown" > press 1 (Not lead).
   Or press on a picture and drag down to tick a run. Arrows keep your ticks; Esc clears.
   Quick flow: one line per product now (switch "Compact rows" off for the big rows). Look at the graph
   (top of the right panel), press 1-6, the next one's graph is already there. The rest of a long shelf
   loads as you scroll - or "Show all" at the bottom.
