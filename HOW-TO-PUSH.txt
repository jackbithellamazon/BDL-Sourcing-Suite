STEP 2 · the Worker, for the price charts     2 minutes, and it cannot lose anything
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cloudflare → Workers & Pages → bdl-sourcing → Edit code
Select all the code, paste in 2026-09-16-BDL-SOURCING-WORKER-v2.js → Deploy
Then REFRESH the Sourcing app once.

Why it is safe:
 · Your Keepa key is NOT in that file. It lives in the Worker's secret,
   KEEPA_API_KEY, and editing code never touches secrets.
 · The whole change is two lines: 'graphimage' added to the allowed list, and
   an image is passed through as an image instead of text. Everything else,
   including who is allowed to call it, is identical.
 · If anything at all looks wrong: paste ROLLBACK-old-worker-v1.js back in and
   Deploy. Cloudflare also keeps every past version under Deployments, so you
   can roll back there in one click without any file.
 · Nothing else in the app depends on this step. Skip it and everything works,
   you just press K for the chart instead of seeing it in the panel.
