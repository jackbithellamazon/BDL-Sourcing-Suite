£ per € fetching… this browser

Used to turn an EU buy price into pounds (Rule 1). Comes from the ECB every 6 hours, rounded up so the maths errs on the safe side. Type over it if you want a different rate.

Reasons for a No shared

What a VA picks from when a lead is a No. Keep them short. The rules are refined from these after the trial.

Discounts · brand stores & retailers shared

What a brand's own store, or a retailer that price-matches Amazon, gives on top. The high-ticket rule uses this to show "→ score with Currys 5%" and to decide how much discount a brand can realistically give. Full price only means the code does not work on a sale item unless a sale rate is set.

Rule 4 · what counts as 0% VAT shared

Tea and coffee are 0% VAT. A lead is worked at 0% when its title or category contains a word from the first list and none from the second, so coffee beans is 0% and coffee machine is 20%. Whole words, comma-separated. A VA's own 0% / 20% click on a lead always wins over these lists.

Blacklists

Central. Applied to every run of every filter and brand, after the Keepa export is read — so nothing has to change inside Keepa.

ASIN blacklist shared

These products never show again, on any run. Added with the ⃠ button on a lead — a reason is required. Anyone can remove one here; the removal carries your name.

Brand blacklist Jack approves

Whole brands we cannot sell, such as HP. A VA requests it with a reason; it shows as pending until Jack approves. Once approved every product of that brand is dropped from every run. Pending brands keep showing with a PENDING chip so nothing is lost while Jack decides.
